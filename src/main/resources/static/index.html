About Payanam

Building the next generation of public transport tracking systems

Payanam is a smart bus tracking and ticket booking platform designed to simplify public transit. We merge secure digital ticket purchases with immediate collector scans and real-time route progress.

No more carrying exact change, losing paper tickets, or waiting at stops indefinitely. Simply purchase your digital pass online, view the active sequence updates from the collector, and show your confirmation ticket.
