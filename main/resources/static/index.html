About Us

Payanam is a modern bus ticket booking and real-time tracking application that provides a convenient and efficient way for users to book bus tickets online. Our platform offers a wide range of bus routes, schedules, and ticket options to cater to the diverse needs of our users.

With a user-friendly interface and secure payment options, Payanam aims to make the bus ticket booking process seamless and hassle-free for everyone. You can track your bus in real-time using our application, avoiding the need to carry exact change. Just show your online ticket to the collector and enjoy a hassle-free travel experience.
